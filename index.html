My Approach

I don't just complete the task. I look at where the task sits in the store.

When I edit a product, I check how it fits the collection, navigation, variants, customer-facing information, and the surrounding catalog. When I research a product, I organize the information so the owner can make the final decision. The goal is a cleaner operation, not a longer task list.
