Workflow

A clear process from raw information to store-ready work.

My workflow is designed for e-commerce tasks where small details connect. I research first, organize the information, execute inside the store, then review what changed.

01 · UNDERSTAND

Define the task

I clarify the objective, current store setup, required output, and any rules that should not be changed.

Output: task scope + priorities
02 · AUDIT

Check what already exists

I review products, collections, variants, navigation, content, and existing data before changing anything.

Output: issues + opportunities
03 · RESEARCH

Gather the right information

For product or supplier work, I compare relevant details rather than treating the first available result as the answer.

Output: organized research
04 · ORGANIZE

Structure the data

I prepare product information, collections, tags, variants, spreadsheets, and supporting records in a consistent format.

Output: clean working data
05 · EXECUTE

Make the store changes

I apply the approved changes inside Shopify or the agreed workflow, keeping the storefront and backend aligned.

Output: completed store work
06 · REVIEW

QA before handoff

I check the final result for missing information, inconsistent product data, broken links, layout issues, or overlooked details.

Output: reviewed result + update

For Product Sourcing

I organize supplier, pricing, MOQ, lead-time, specifications, shipping, and other available evidence into a comparison. The business owner makes the final purchasing decision.

Product→Supplier→Compare→Recommend

For Shopify Operations

I work from the catalog outward: product data → collections → navigation → storefront presentation → operational checks. That helps prevent isolated fixes that create new inconsistencies elsewhere.

Product Data→Collections→Navigation→Storefront→QA
What I Document

Changes should be easy to understand after the task is finished.

Depending on the project, that can mean a product sheet, supplier comparison, task tracker, change log, inventory update, or short handoff summary. The point is to leave the operation easier to continue, not dependent on memory.
